================================================================================
    DOF-5 STAGE TELEMETRY REFERENCE — Verified Requestable Opcodes
================================================================================

This file documents every read opcode the Juno chip on the DOF-5 stage
responded to during the smoke test (phase 4 opcode probe). These will be
used to assemble the final latency benchmark.

All opcodes listed below had 3/3 successful responses. They can be read
at any time (including during motion) by sending a CAN request.

  CAN bus:      can0, 1 Mbps
  TX_ID:        0x600
  RX_ID:        0x580
  Axis:         0

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Opcode | Label                | Response bytes | Typical latency (per read)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  0x37   | GetActualPosition    | 4              | ~370-380 us (median)
  0x35   | GetEventStatus       | 2              | ~350-375 us
  0xA6   | GetActivityStatus    | 4              | ~340-360 us
  0xA7   | GetSignalStatus      | 6              | ~340-360 us
  0xAD   | GetActualVelocity    | 6              | ~390-430 us
  0x1E   | GetTargetPosition    | 6              | ~430-460 us
  0x4A   | GetCommandedPosition | 6              | ~380-420 us
  0xB6   | GetMotorCommand      | 2              | ~315-345 us
  0x4B   | GetIntegrationStep   | 6              | ~380-440 us

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
COMBINED READ POLLING

  Polling ALL 8 verified opcodes in a single sweep takes ~3.3-3.5 ms per
  sample. This was demonstrated during the motion smoke test where position
  + all extra opcodes were read at each trace point.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
COMMAND LATENCY (SetPosition + Update)

  Median:  ~719-727 us
  p95:     ~756-778 us
  p99:     ~859-884 us

  Measured across 1000 command-path round trips per run (no motion).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MOVE-TO-SETTLE (50 um, 1.0 mm/s, 20.0 mm/s²)

  Median settle time from command: ~145.7 ms
  Range:                          ~143.5-172.8 ms
  Settle criterion:               within ±15 nm of target for 5 ms continuous

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
NOTES FOR THE LATENCY BENCHMARK

  - GetActualPosition is the primary telemetry for polling position at
    high rate (~370 us per read).

  - GetActualVelocity and GetTargetPosition can provide richer state
    during motion — useful for deriving motion-start and in-flight
    corrections without waiting for full settle.

  - GetActivityStatus and GetEventStatus may indicate motion-in-progress
    vs. idle state — useful for detecting when a move finishes without
    polling position.

  - The smoke test was run with 50 um moves. Larger moves will take
    proportionally longer. Smaller focus corrections may complete faster.
